Great Wall of China

  • Location: Northern China
  • Built: 7th century BCE onwards
  • Length: Over 21,000 kilometers
  • Significance: Built to protect Chinese states and empires against invasions.

Taj Mahal

  • Location: Agra,Uttar Pradesh,India
  • Built: 1632-1653 CE
  • Material: Ivory-white marble inlaid with semi-precious stones.
  • Significance: Commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan as a mausoleum for his wife, Mumtaz Mahal, it is widely regarded as the finest example of Mughal architecture.

Petra

  • Location: Ma'an Governorate, Jordan
  • Built: Around the 5th century BCE
  • Material: Rose-red sandstone cliffs
  • Significance: Served as the capital of the Nabataean Kingdom, famous for its rock-cut architecture (such as Al-Khazneh, or "The Treasury") and advanced water conduit system.

Colosseum

  • Location: Rome, Italy
  • Built: 70-80 CE
  • Capacity: Held between 50,000 and 80,000 spectators
  • Significance: The largest ancient amphitheater ever built, used for gladiatorial contests, public spectacles, and mock sea battles under the Roman Empire.

Chichén Itzá

  • Location: Yucatan State,Mexico
  • Built: Roughly 600-1200 CE
  • Key Structure: El Castillo (Temple of Kukulcán), a 30-meter step pyramid
  • Significance: A major pre-Columbian city built by the Maya civilization.

Machu Picchu

  • Location: Cusco Region, Peru (Andes Mountains)
  • Built: Mid-15th century (around 1450 CE)
  • Elevation: 2,430 meters (7,970 feet) above sea level
  • Significance: A 15th-century Inca citadel built using dry-stone walls without mortar.

Christ the Redeemer

  • Location: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (atop Mount Corcovado)
  • Built: 1922-1931
  • Length: 30 meters (98 feet) tall
  • Significance: Designed by French sculptor Paul Landowski built with reinforced concrete and soapstone.
